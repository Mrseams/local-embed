Employee Leave Policy

Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.

Unused leave of up to five days may be carried over into the following year. Any balance above five days is forfeited on 31 March.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Employees may take up to ten paid sick days per year; absences longer than three consecutive days require a medical certificate.

Parental leave is available to all employees after six months of service, with sixteen weeks paid at full salary.
